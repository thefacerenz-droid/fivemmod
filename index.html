Honest answers

Is ModsHub free?

Yes. The full workflow, free mod library, and FPS tools are free. Premium mods and faster downloads are in ModsHub Plus, but you do not need it to get value.

Will ModsHub work on the FiveM server I play on?

On almost every FiveM server, yes. ModsHub is client side — your graphics, car skins, and UI changes happen locally. The exception is servers with pure level enabled. Always check server rules.

What does the FPS optimization actually do?

It lets you tune render distance, object density, shadows, particles, fog, glare, grass, and weather toggles — all in one place. How many frames you get back depends on your rig.

How does installing actually work?

You hit install and ModsHub drops the files in the right place. The only manual step is if you have multiple GTA installs — pick the correct game folder once in Settings.
